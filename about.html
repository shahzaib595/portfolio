About Me

The person
behind the code.

Developer by discipline. Designer by obsession. Six years building products that refuse to be forgettable.

I started in design — obsessing over the pixel, the kerning, the affordance. Then I discovered that writing code gave me the same feeling as a perfectly executed layout, and I never looked back.

Today I operate across the full stack — from WebGL shaders to database query plans. I believe great digital products appear when design and engineering are treated as one craft, not two departments that throw files over a wall.

I've shipped products for Y Combinator-backed startups, global design agencies, and independent founders. Six years in, the constant is this: constraints are the most creative tool we have.

When I'm not building, I'm writing about creative technology, contributing to open-source tooling, and finding the places where WebGL makes the browser feel alive.
